# Goal: parcel lockers in the lobby

Couriers leave parcels at the front desk, and the desk is open from eight to eight. Residents who work late cannot collect until the next day, and the desk keeps about 140 parcels on a busy week. The building put twelve lockers in the lobby, and nothing tells a resident which door holds their parcel.

## Who it is for

- Residents, who want to know a parcel has arrived and to collect it at any hour.
- Front desk staff, who take over parcels nobody collects.

Couriers use the locker screen as they do in other buildings. Returns to the courier are not part of this.

## Done means

- A resident hears within a minute that a parcel is in a locker, with the code that opens it.
- No parcel sits in a locker for more than three days.
