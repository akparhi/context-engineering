# Goal: holds on books that are out

Members cannot reserve a book that is out. They ask at the desk, and the desk keeps a paper list for each book. At the central branch those lists hold 312 books today. Two members can be promised the same copy, and a returned copy can go back on the open shelf while someone still waits for it.

## Who it is for

- Members, who place a hold from the book page and pick the copy up from the hold shelf.
- Desk librarians, who shelve returned copies and cancel holds when a member asks at the desk.

Branch managers are not part of this. Reports on which books people wait for come in a later plan.

## Done means

- A member can hold any book whose copies are all out, without asking at the desk.
- Each returned copy goes to the first member in line, and that member hears about it the same day.
- A member who does not collect within three opening days loses the copy to the next member.
- 8 in 10 holds end with the member picking the copy up, read from the weekly hold report.
